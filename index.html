What we do

Complete elevator services

Whether you are building new, upgrading an old lift or just need it to keep running, everything is handled under one roof.

New installation

Site survey, shaft planning, supply of the elevator and full installation, testing and handover for new buildings and villas.

FAQ

Questions we hear often

How long does a new elevator installation take?

It depends on the elevator type, number of floors and shaft readiness. We give you a clear timeline in the quotation after the site survey.

Do you service elevators that you did not install?

Yes, in most cases. We inspect the lift first and then recommend a maintenance plan or any repairs it needs.

How often should an elevator be serviced?

Regular servicing, usually monthly or quarterly depending on use and local rules, keeps the lift safe and avoids costly breakdowns.

Can an old elevator be upgraded instead of replaced?

Often yes. Modernizing the controller, drive, doors and cabin can give you a near-new ride at a lower cost than full replacement.

What should I do if someone is stuck in the lift?

Ask them to stay calm, press the alarm button and wait. Call our emergency breakdown line immediately and do not try to open the doors by force.
